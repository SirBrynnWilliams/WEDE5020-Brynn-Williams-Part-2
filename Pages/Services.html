Our Adoption Services

What we Offer

Services Overview

Jahs Adoption Centre offers a range of services designed to encourage and inspire potential child adopters throughout their journey. Our goal is to ensure every child finds a loving home through a professional and caring process.

Services We Provide

Adoption Process Breakdown

We provide a complete overview of the adoption process to help you acquire a child of your choice. Our team guides you through each step, from the initial application to the final placement, ensuring you feel supported at all times.

Legal Standards and Requirements

We ensure that all adoptions meet the strict legal requirements for child safety in South Africa. Our staff assists couples in understanding the legal documentation needed to provide a secure future for their new family member.

Counselling and Guidance Sessions

Professional counselling is available for new couples who are adopting children. We also provide specialized children guidance sessions on weekends during selected time intervals to help with emotional transitions and family bonding.

Adoption Centre Volunteering

Beyond our adoption services, we welcome community members to get involved through our volunteering program. This allows the local community to support our children in Walmer and assist in creating a learning-rich environment.

Jahs Adoption Centre

Founded in 2018 in Gqeberha, Jahs Adoption Centre is a non-profit organisation dedicated to providing safe housing and finding loving forever homes for vulnerable children.
